This module was developed because standard Odoo does not allow overlapping leaves.

For legislations where being sick during vacation counts as sick day and not a vacation
day, you'd have to cancel the original vacation, and recreate sick leaves / vacation
leaves as necessary.

With this module, you can simply allow overlapping for sick leaves, and add the sick
leave on top of the vacation leave. This will also deduct the sick days taken from the
vacation days taken.

The deducted days are credited back as a regular allocation that is linked to the two
leaves it originates from. It starts on the first day of the overlapped leave, so the
days are available at the time they were lost, and it has no end date, so it does not
expire together with the allocation the days were originally taken from. Withdrawing the
sick leave refuses that allocation again, which takes it out of the balance while
keeping it on record.
